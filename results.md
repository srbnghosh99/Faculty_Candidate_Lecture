To\nanswer this question, this article presents narrative summaries—a current history—\nof all eleven coups attempts in Africa between August 2020 and November\n2022. Introduction\n\nFor six decades, Africa has been one of the most coup-prone regions in the world.\nAccording to the Colpus dataset, a new dataset of coup types and characteristics,1 the region\nhas suﬀered a total of 222 coup attempts since 1946, more than any other world region.2\n\nAs of 2022, 45 of the region’s 54 countries have suﬀered one or more coup attempts.3 The\naverage African state has suﬀered four coup attempts since independence, with Sudan having\nthe most (17), including two just last year.\nAt their historic peak in the 1960s, nearly thirty percent of all African states had suﬀered\none or more coup attempts in the prior two years.
